# Current live evidence and unresolved history

Historical V6 implementation notes. The current product has removed YOLO; see [MiniCPM-only analysis](model-only-analysis.md) and `analysis/src/vlm-coordinator.js` for the active policy.

`two-server-priority-v6` separates the current detector/model findings from incident history. `detection.smoke` no longer turns into `detected` solely because an earlier frame or previous connection confirmed a smoke signal. `incident_history` retains unresolved fire/smoke and validated model hazards. Every reconnect starts a new current detector confirmation, while preserving that history.

Eight ordered, valid quiet detector observations can expire a current proposal. Unknown visibility cannot resolve an incident or establish safety. Gaps, duplicates and degraded images do not advance quiet continuity. Smoke-absent observations are counted independently from flame, so a lighter cannot keep an old smoke signal current. Confirmed fire history is also protected from unknown-quality negative clearance. A mostly unreadable dark frame, including sensor noise plus a few lit pixels, is rejected as insufficient illumination; this never grants adequate visibility.

Native scene evidence requires three distinct, strictly validated, independently frame-bound reports spanning at least two seconds. The bounded pool retains up to twelve reports over eight seconds to accommodate actual report cadence and timestamp jitter. Selection finds an anchor at least two seconds before the latest report, rather than assuming the last three reports always span that interval. Every intervening report must agree with a negative scene; lighter evidence also rejects any intervening separate or uncertain flame, smoke, burning material or occlusion. Every supporting source frame must remain within ten seconds: the scene lease expires by its oldest supporting timestamp, rather than extending old evidence using a newer image. Every second native step schedules a report, keeping three reports fresh without increasing the freshness limit.

Three current rows explicitly reporting no flame, smoke, burning material or occlusion can support a negative scene. A current detector proposal that contradicts that scene produces `unknown / detector_model_disagreement`, with a request for human review. It does not produce a low-risk label. If detector proposals stop, monitoring requires all three supporting reports to follow every detector proposal, including a single fire box or weak smoke. Any new proposal immediately revokes that lease. The incident record remains unresolved for review; current monitoring does not claim that a previous event never happened or that a fire is extinguished.

Supported lighter monitoring retains the reviewed camera-policy and multiframe evidence requirements. Contradictory detector findings request review. Validated material/smoke hazards, reviewed site hazards, supported growth and unavailable views override monitoring. A persisted model hazard remains urgent even before a new valid frame arrives, including through reconnect and camera gaps.

The public page distinguishes processed native frames from valid current scene reports, shows report expiry/no-report states, labels detector signals as detector output, and displays unresolved prior alerts separately. Transport health is never presented as completed scene assessment.

Verification evidence is under `.hermes/darkness-20261024/`. Local suites: 84 JavaScript, 108 Python script and 3 detector tests. Real L40S cadence probe: six valid reports across twelve native steps (room twice, lighter three times, burning wood/smoke once), maximum step 970ms and peak allocated GPU memory about16.63GiB.
